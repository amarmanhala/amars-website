Why FDE?

The future belongs to engineers who turn AI into outcomes.

In an AI-first world, the advantage will not come from simply having access to models. It will come from turning them into useful systems inside real businesses. Forward deployed engineers work at that intersection: close to the problem, close to the people, and responsible for shipping the outcome.

I am fascinated by building things and by the messy path from an idea to something people can actually use. FDE work brings design, engineering, curiosity, and execution together—and that is the future I want to help build.
